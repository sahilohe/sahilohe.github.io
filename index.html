About

I am an aspiring mathematician and technology enthusiast, self-studying mathematics, CS & quantum physics from India. My aim is to understand how things really work: to pursue explanations rather than credentials, and to spend my time on the questions that seem most worth asking.

My thinking is shaped by David Deutsch’s four strands of explanations and by critical rationalism. Knowledge grows through conjecture and criticism, and the explanations worth keeping are the ones hardest to vary. I am drawn to epistemology, formal systems, computation and quantum theory, and to the strange loops where mathematics, minds and physics meet.

I learn by building. Each project is an experiment in understanding: a way to test ideas, refine the explanations behind them, and develop better mental models.

Hire me for doing math, talk & teach explanations, or for building websites.
